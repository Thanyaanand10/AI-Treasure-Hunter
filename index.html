You are a treasure hunter on a slippery island. An AI will learn to do the same job using maths.

  1. Build an island with walls, traps and 💎.
  2. Train the AI and watch Value Iteration spread value from the treasure.
  3. Play yourself, earn ⭐ by matching the AI, or race it.
